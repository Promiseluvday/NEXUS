What changed from v1 and why

  1. Status shows who set it and when PROPOSAL P-FB-1. Status is a recorded event by a named person, never derived by the system (D-020, D-024).
  2. An unassessed pilot report is a flag, not a status change PROPOSAL P-FB-2. NX-203 keeps its last engineer-set status and carries a "check before dispatch" flag. Operators whose procedure grounds on a tech-log entry can switch pilot_report_effect.
  3. Blocker holder follows the item's real holder. NX-101's requisition sits at the CO approval step, so the holder is Command, not Procurement (matches part-request RQ-2).
  4. Engineers mark what blocks release PROPOSAL P-FB-3. The board shows the longest-held blocker plus "+N more".
  5. Counts say what they count: tiles count aircraft; the MEL tile also gives the number of deferrals.
  6. One label set, long and short forms (D-025, open item O-7). Desktop uses long, mobile uses short.
  7. "In check" = blue PROPOSAL P-FB-4 (D-091 has no entry for it).
  8. Mobile lists all 6 tails, worst first. v1 dropped NX-201 and NX-202.
  9. Offline values are stamped "at 08:42"; held times are frozen at the snapshot, not ticking as if live.
  10. Hours shown as hh:mm from stored minutes; format is an operator setting.
  11. All tap targets ≥ 44 px (D-093): chips, nav, tiles, buttons.
